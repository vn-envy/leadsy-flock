## Edit rhythm (YC, not a webinar)

- Cut on verbs. Never hold a diagram without a moving light.
- Music: 110–120 BPM, percussion + tanpura or a dry kick. Duck under Hindi VO.
- Burned-in captions, centre-safe, English. One line at a time.
- Every 8 seconds: a new picture or a number.
- If a shot does not say cost, speed, effort, YES, or never-autopost — cut it.
